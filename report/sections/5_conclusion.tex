\section{Conclusion}
We re-implemented soft-DTW from scratch, verified it against exhaustive enumeration of alignments, and reproduced the paper's barycenter, $k$-means and nearest-centroid results on 10--12 UCR datasets: the main claims hold, as do the trends in $\gamma$.
